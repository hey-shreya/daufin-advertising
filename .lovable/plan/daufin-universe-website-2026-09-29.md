# Daufin Universe Website

## Goal
Transform the current single-page agency site into a premium editorial, multi-page Daufin experience built around “Where Brands Meet Culture,” with Our Work as the central destination.

## Pages and navigation
- Home: cinematic introduction, featured work, service overview, and horizontal campaign timeline
- About: Who We Are, positioning, and agency story
- Services: Branded Entertainment, Brand Integration, Digital Campaigns, Experiential, and Media & Entertainment
- Our Work: filterable campaign index for All Work, Brand Integrations, Digital, TV & Film, and Experiential
- Seven individual case studies: BRU × Aisha, Micromax × Housefull, Zen × Break Ke Baad, Monster × Transformers, Godrej × Roy, Shree Cement × Tanhaji, and WOW Skin Science × Badhaai Do
- Approach: The Daufin Method
- Team
- Journal
- Contact

## Visual direction
- Premium editorial art direction using black, off-white, and a signature deep-purple accent
- Large expressive typography, restrained layouts, generous whitespace, campaign-led imagery, and subtle transitions
- Minimal navigation with a polished mobile menu
- Interactive work tiles and a horizontal campaign timeline rather than a timeline-led website
- Cohesive generated campaign imagery where original campaign assets are unavailable

## Experience details
- Shared navigation and footer across every page
- Clear links between work tiles, case studies, services, and contact
- Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile
- Functional work filters, navigation, contact form feedback, and WhatsApp link
- Preserve supplied business claims and avoid inventing additional statistics or contact details

## Technical details
- Use React Router routes with shared layout and reusable editorial page components
- Keep all visual values in semantic design tokens
- Store campaign/service content in a shared data module so listings and detail pages remain consistent
- Use locally imported generated imagery rather than remote image links
- Update page titles and social metadata for the Daufin Universe positioning
- Verify core navigation, work filtering, a case-study path, and contact interaction in the running preview
